Method & sources

  • Data: ERA5 reanalysis daily precipitation totals via the Open-Meteo Historical Weather API.
  • Locations: Bergen (60.39°N, 5.32°E) and Gijón (43.53°N, 5.66°W).
  • Period: 1995–2025 daily totals; climate normal = 1995–2024 (30-year window). 2026 year-to-date through —.
  • Rainy day: any day with > 1.0 mm of precipitation. The 1 mm threshold removes trace events that bias counts upward for damp climates.
  • Consecutive streak: uninterrupted run of rainy days. Single-day rains count as a streak of length 1.
